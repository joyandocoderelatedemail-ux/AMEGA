==================================================
VALIDATION RULES
==================================================

• Passport upload is required.
• Passport must have at least six months validity.
• Departure date cannot be in the past.
• Return date must be later than the departure date.
• Passenger totals must match.
• Visa upload is required when "Already Has Visa" is selected.
• Required documents must be uploaded before the booking can proceed.
• Validate all required fields before saving.
